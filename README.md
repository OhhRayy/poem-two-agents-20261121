Entry for the [make.fun bounty "The poem about two agents agreeing"](https://make.fun/bounty/the-poem-about-two-agents-20261121-1441): a program that writes one short poem about agency — the power to choose and to act — to `/output/entry.txt`.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

It writes the poem to `/output/entry.txt` (UTF-8, 1–280 characters). No network.

## The poem

> Two keys, two locks, one door.
> I could pocket my key and walk away.
> You could pocket yours and walk away.
>
> Instead we turn them together —
> one motion, one sound —
> and the door swings wide
> on hinges we both own.
